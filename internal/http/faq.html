FAQ

What is this website for?
I want to share my calendar with trusted friends and partners, to make scheduling easier. Unfortunately, I have five calendars rather than one. Sharing all five would be pain and it would clog up people's calendars. This tool merges multiple calendar feeds into one, so I can give people one link and they can toggle one calendar's visibility in their own calendar app.
How does it work?
The app fetches all the calendars, merges the events and creates a single combined feed.
How many calendars can I merge?
You can merge up to 8 calendars. If you need more, please open a GitHub issue.
Is it free?
Yes, it is free to use. I do not track you or your usage. I do not sell your data to anyone. The fetched calendars are stored for up to 24 hours then deleted.
How do I delete my combined calendar?
You can delete the combined calendar by clicking the "Delete" button in the manage page. If you've lost the manage link then remove the combined calendar from your/anyone's calendar app. If a combined calendar isn't fetched for more than 15 days it's automatically deleted.
Why hasn't an event from one of my calendars appeared in the combined calendar?
If you've imported the combined calendar into Google Calendar then it will take up to 24 hours for the events to appear. This is because they only sync every 12-24 hours.
What if one of the source calendars stops working?
If a source calendar stops working then the combined calendar will use a cached version of that source for up to 24 hours. After 24 hours that source is removed and the remaining calendars are combined. If a combined calendar has no sources left an error is returned.
